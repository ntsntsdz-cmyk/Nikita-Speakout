2. try to + infinitive vs try + -ing

📝 Mini explanation

try to + infinitive = make an effort / attempt to do something (often difficult).
Example: I’m trying to watch a movie in English.

try + -ing = experiment / do something to see what happens.
Example: Try watching films with subtitles – it might help.

Common mistake: “I’m trying watching…” → correct: “I’m trying to watch…”

2A. Choose the correct form 0/5
2B. Rewrite – correct the mistake 0/3

Rewrite the whole sentence correctly.

1. Now I’m trying watching a movie in English.

Now I’m trying to watch a movie in English.

2. Try to going to the gym if you want to feel better.

Try going to the gym if you want to feel better.

3. She’s trying being more open with strangers.

She’s trying to be more open with strangers.
